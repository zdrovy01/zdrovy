FAQ
When is the app launching?+
We're currently in development. Follow us on Instagram or TikTok for updates — we'll announce the launch date there first.
Which stores are supported?+
At launch we're targeting Biedronka, Lidl, Kaufland, Auchan and Żabka — the stores most people in Poland already shop at.
Is there a free version?+
Yes — the free plan will always exist. You get core features with no time limit. Pro adds unlimited recipes, all stores, and the weekly budget planner.
How are prices kept up to date?+
We pull prices directly from store databases and promotional leaflets on a daily basis, so what you see reflects what you'll actually pay at the checkout.
What platforms will it be on?+
iOS and Android at launch. A web version may follow based on demand.
